Product Information

Product Description

The MAC Studio Fix Conceal and Correct Palette brings together concealing and color-correcting shades in one compact palette. Use the correcting shades to neutralize dark circles and discoloration, then conceal and brighten with the matching skin tone shades for smooth, natural-looking coverage.

Ingredients

Example ingredients list: Talc, Dimethicone, Mica, Zinc Stearate, Caprylyl Glycol, Tocopheryl Acetate, Iron Oxides (CI 77491, CI 77492, CI 77499), Titanium Dioxide (CI 77891). Please check the product packaging for the full and current ingredients list.

How to Use

  1. Start with clean, moisturized skin.
  2. Apply a thin layer of the corrector shade to areas of discoloration and blend with a brush or sponge.
  3. Apply the concealer shade that matches your skin tone over the corrected area.
  4. Blend the edges and set with powder if desired.
